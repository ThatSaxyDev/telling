## Design Context

### Users
Developers and small app teams reviewing logs, errors, sessions and analytics from their instrumented applications.

### Brand Personality
Precise, technical and focused. Copy should explain what a screen helps someone do, without grand claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve Telling's existing dark dashboard, black-and-white palette, Syne and Urbanist typography, fine grid, angular framing, and severity colors. The creator accepted this direction and revised the first headline to “See what's happening in your app.”

### Design Principles
- Real, populated production screens lead the artwork; omit empty Funnels.
- Preserve app screen proportions and use genuine tablet layouts.
- Keep short, concrete headlines and readable UI.
- Export each store slot independently and verify against an independent size table.
- Keep screenshot staging separate from the shipping app and live account data.
