## Description (4000 chars max)
Deploy tactical intelligence for your mobile applications with Telling.

Telling is the ultimate command center for developers who demand total situational awareness of their application's performance. Designed with a unique tactical interface, it turns raw data into actionable intelligence.

**KEY CAPABILITIES:**

• **Values Visualization:** Monitor your application's heartbeat in real-time. Stream logs, events, and errors as they happen with millisecond precision.

• **Crash Intelligence:** Don't just catch errors—neutralize them. Get detailed crash reports with stack traces, device metadata, and user context to resolve critical issues rapidly.

• **Tactical User Tracking:** Understand your user base. Track active sessions, user journeys, and engagement metrics to optimize the user experience.

• **Funnel Analysis:** Visualize conversion flows and identify drop-off points in your application's key processes.

• **Performance Monitoring:** Keep your app running at peak efficiency with detailed performance metrics and health stati.

• **Secure & Private:** Your data is classified. All transmission is encrypted, ensuring your intelligence remains for your eyes only.

Telling integrates seamlessly with the `telling_logger` SDK for Flutter, providing a complete ecosystem for application monitoring.
